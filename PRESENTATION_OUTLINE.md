# Hackathon Presentation Outline & Demo Script

## Slide Outline (7 Slides)
1. **Title:** Kaagaz Path – Navigating Indian Government Paperwork with Graph Theory.
2. **The Problem:** Rejection loops, circular paperwork dependencies, and citizen frustration.
3. **The Solution:** Deterministic Directed Acyclic Graph (DAG) dependency solver.
4. **Core Innovation:** Kahn's Topological Sorting algorithm applied to administrative law.
5. **Inclusivity & Accessibility:** Multilingual (EN/HI/KN), Text-to-Speech, High Contrast, Zero PII.
6. **Architecture & Extensibility:** Decoupled rule engine, state-specific adapter architecture.
7. **Future Roadmap:** DigiLocker API integration, CSC kiosk offline mode, voice-first IVR.

---

## 3-Minute Demo Script
- **0:00 - 0:45:** Introduce the common problem of blocked government certificate applications.
- **0:45 - 1:30:** Open Kaagaz Path, select "Income Certificate Readiness", and demonstrate the interactive DAG flowchart.
- **1:30 - 2:15:** Switch languages (Kannada/Hindi), demonstrate TTS audio reading, and show real-time unblocking when marking a prerequisite as available.
- **2:15 - 3:00:** Show the generated legal petition draft, print preview, and summarize the social impact.
